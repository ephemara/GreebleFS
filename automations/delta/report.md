2. The project still presents itself like a starter template at the top level instead of a shippable product.

4. Screenshot workflows need end-to-end validation as a product feature, not just a component.

6. Cross-panel workflows may still rely on tribal knowledge.

## Current Execution Bias

- First priority is repository workflow completion and product-level release framing.
- After that, the lane should move through asset-management flows and screenshots.

## Ship Standard For This Lane

- A workflow is not complete unless it is understandable, integrated, and validated.
- New user confusion counts as a ship blocker.
- Template leftovers count as product debt and should be eliminated early.

## Goal

- Turn unfinished product surface area into validated, integrated, shippable behavior over repeated hourly runs.
